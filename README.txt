ELDERSBURN LODGE WEBSITE

Open index.html in a browser to preview the site.

FILES
- index.html: page content
- styles.css: design and mobile layout
- script.js: calendar and enquiry form behaviour
- assets/: lodge photographs extracted from your supplied document

HOW TO PUBLISH FREE
1. Create a free Netlify account.
2. Drag the entire eldersburn-lodge folder onto Netlify's deployment page.
3. Netlify gives you a free web address immediately.

HOW TO MAKE THE CONTACT FORM LIVE
The current form is a safe demo and does not transmit personal data.
Create a free Formspree form, then replace the submit handler in script.js with the endpoint they provide, or connect the site to your preferred booking platform.

HOW TO UPDATE BOOKINGS
Open script.js and edit bookedRanges near the top. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD.
Example: ['2026-08-14','2026-08-18']

IMPORTANT
The calendar currently contains illustrative booked dates only. Replace these with your real availability before publishing.
