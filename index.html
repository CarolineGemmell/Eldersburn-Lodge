Welcome to Eldersburn

A comfortable base for a memorable Highland break.

Set among mature trees and rolling hills, Eldersburn Lodge blends the warmth of a traditional timber lodge with bright, modern living spaces.

Enjoy unhurried breakfasts, peaceful afternoons and cosy evenings together. The lodge is well placed for exploring the Loch Tay area, local villages, walking routes and some of Scotland’s most beautiful countryside.
